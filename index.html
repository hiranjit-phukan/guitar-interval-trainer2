Intervals are the distance between notes

An interval describes the musical distance between a root note and another note. On guitar, semitones are the physical distance between adjacent frets.

1 · Choose the root Example: G
2 · Count semitones G → G♯ → A → A♯ → B = 4
3 · Name the interval 4 semitones = Major 3rd
